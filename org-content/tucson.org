Many native Sonoran plants can appear spontaneously in a garden,
identify them before pulling them!

** January
*** To remove
**** London rocket (Sisymbrium irio)
Winter annual. Pull young rosettes and remove before seed production.

[[file:images/london_rocket.png]]

London rocket can produce thousands of seeds; Arizona Invasive Plants
recommends manual removal while young, at the basal-rosette stage.

Source: [[https://azinvasiveplants.arizona.edu/invasive-plant/london-rocket][Arizona Invasive Plants — London rocket]]

**** Sweet clover (Melilotus indica)
Remove unwanted winter annuals while soil is moist and plants are small.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/publication/january-monthly-gardening-guide-pima-county][UA Cooperative Extension — January Monthly Gardening Guide for Pima County]]

**** Stinknet (Oncosiphon pilulifer)
Monitor newly emerging rosettes after winter rain; bag pulled plants.

Stinknet is a rapidly spreading invasive annual in southern Arizona.
University of Arizona guidance emphasizes early detection, repeated
control, and preventing seed spread. Do not burn it.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/publication/stinknet-southern-arizona][UA Cooperative Extension — Stinknet in Southern Arizona]]

*** To sustain plant
**** Parry's penstemon (Penstemon parryi)
Native Sonoran Desert spring wildflower; excellent for hummingbirds.

[[file:images/parrys_penstemon.webp]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-04/Native_Plants_Tucson_Area_HGT.pdf][Pima County Master Gardeners — Native Plants of the Tucson Area]]

**** Desert willow (Chilopsis linearis)
Native tree for appropriate wash-like or moister landscape positions.

Source: [[https://apps.cals.arizona.edu/arboretum/taxon.aspx?id=72][UA Campus Arboretum — Desert willow]]

** February
*** To remove
**** London rocket (Sisymbrium irio)
Often conspicuous and flowering now; pull before pods mature.

Source: [[https://azinvasiveplants.arizona.edu/invasive-plant/london-rocket][Arizona Invasive Plants — London rocket]]

**** Stinknet (Oncosiphon pilulifer)
Remove flowering plants before seed set and bag them.

[[file:images/stinknet_bloom.png]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/publication/stinknet-southern-arizona][UA Cooperative Extension — Stinknet in Southern Arizona]]

**** Sweet clover (Melilotus indica)
Remove before flowering and seed production if unwanted.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/publication/february-monthly-gardening-guide-pima-county][UA Cooperative Extension — February Monthly Gardening Guide for Pima County]]

*** To sustain plant
**** Parry's penstemon (Penstemon parryi)
Spring bloom approaches; protect seedlings and avoid confusing them with mustard rosettes.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-04/Native_Plants_Tucson_Area_HGT.pdf][Pima County Master Gardeners — Native Plants of the Tucson Area]]

**** Fairy duster (Calliandra eriophylla)
Native shrub; a good time to establish container-grown plants before extreme heat.

[[file:images/fairy_duster.jpg]]

Source: [[https://aznps.com/the-plant-list/?species=calliandra+eriophylla][Arizona Native Plant Society — Fairy duster]]

** March
*** To remove
**** London rocket (Sisymbrium irio)
Pull before the long slender pods mature.

Source: [[https://azinvasiveplants.arizona.edu/invasive-plant/london-rocket][Arizona Invasive Plants — London rocket]]

**** Stinknet (Oncosiphon pilulifer)
Flowering is a critical control window; bag plants and do not let them seed.

[[file:images/stinknet_bloom.png]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/stinknet][UA Cooperative Extension — Stinknet]]

**** Buffelgrass (Cenchrus ciliaris)
Inspect landscaped and disturbed areas; remove before seed dispersal where feasible.

Buffelgrass and other invasive grasses can become serious wildfire
fuels. Prioritize removal around structures and in defensible-space
zones. Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

*** To sustain plant
**** Parry's penstemon (Penstemon parryi)
Major spring display.

[[file:images/parrys_penstemon.webp]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/sites/default/files/2026-04/Native_Plants_Tucson_Area_HGT.pdf][Pima County Master Gardeners — Native Plants of the Tucson Area]]

**** Desert marigold (Baileya multiradiata)
Native, drought-tolerant wildflower with a long bloom season.

Source: [[https://aznps.com/the-plant-list/?species=baileya+multiradiata][Arizona Native Plant Society — Desert marigold]]

Inspect before seed dispersal; cured grass is highly flammable.

Buffelgrass and other invasive grasses can become serious wildfire

zones.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

*** To sustain plant
**** Desert willow (Chilopsis linearis)

** July
*** To remove
**** Palmer amaranth / carelessweed (Amaranthus palmeri)
Summer annual; remove before mature seed production.

Palmer amaranth is native to Arizona, but it can still be an unwanted
garden weed; "remove" here means remove where it is unwanted, not
eradicate the native species from the landscape.

[[file:images/palmer_amaranth.webp]]

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

**** Puncturevine (Tribulus terrestris)
Continue removing flowering and fruiting plants.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

**** Horse purslane (Trianthema portulacastrum)
Remove unwanted mats before seed production.

Source: [[https://extension.arizona.edu/topics/weeds][UA Cooperative Extension — Weeds]]

*** To sustain plant
**** Saguaro (Carnegiea gigantea)
Iconic Sonoran native; do not overwater established plants.

[[file:images/saguaro.jpg]]

Source: [[https://www.inaturalist.org/observations/258181216][iNaturalist — Saguaro observation in Saguaro National Park, Tucson]]

**** Desert willow (Chilopsis linearis)
Native summer-flowering tree.

Source: [[https://apps.cals.arizona.edu/arboretum/taxon.aspx?id=72][UA Campus Arboretum — Desert willow]]
